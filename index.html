Who We Are

The Uplifted Woman Prayer Conclave is a safe, non-denominational space where women from every background and belief gather to pray, encourage one another, and be reminded that they don't carry their burdens alone.

“God is within her, she will not fall.” Psalm 46:5

The Preamble of The Uplifted Woman

The uplifted woman is not simply a woman who has risen in status, acquired possessions, gained visibility or achieved professional success. In the economy of the Kingdom, true elevation is measured by conformity to Christ, faithfulness to God, stewardship of grace, service to humanity and the capacity to become a blessing to others.

We therefore pray not merely that women will go higher, but that they will become deeper.

Not merely that doors will open, but that they will possess the wisdom to walk through the right doors.

Not merely that they will be celebrated, but that they will remain faithful when nobody is watching.

Not merely that they will have influence, but that their influence will be governed by truth, character, humility and love.

The prayer of the uplifted woman is ultimately a prayer that Christ will be formed in her.

Our Founder & Spiritual Covering

Rev. Acheampong E. S. Builderman

Pastor · Author · Transformational Leader

“A pastor with a heart for women”

Reverend Acheampong E. S. Builderman is a theologian with a New Testament prophetic voice, a growth thinker, accomplished author, pastor, management strategist and educational consultant, based in Accra, Ghana. He serves as Missions Director and Lead Pastor of The Thanksgiving Place Ministries Incorporated, combining biblical teaching, pastoral leadership and strategic thinking to inspire personal growth and institutional transformation.

A prolific author with 10 published books and many more yet to be released, his writing spans Christian faith, leadership, personal development, African transformation and education. Since 2018 he has trained and been mentored at the Billy Graham Training Centre in Asheville, North Carolina, deepening his commitment to effective Christian leadership.

Educated in the United Kingdom, he holds an MBA from Anglia Ruskin University, Chelmsford, plus certificates in Management Consulting from Emory University and Innovation Management from the Rotterdam School of Management. He is currently a Doctor of Business Administration candidate at Walsh College, Michigan.

His work is distinctive for integrating Christian theology, African development and institutional leadership — creating a platform where faith, knowledge, strategy and social responsibility converge. His experience spans pastoral ministry, management consulting, civil society leadership, youth mentorship, women and girls' empowerment, and United Nations SDG initiatives since 2013.

Across these diverse fields runs one consistent thread: a deep commitment to developing people, strengthening institutions and contributing to a more purposeful and transformed society — leadership measured by the lives and institutions it transforms.

Rev. Builderman is married to Lady Christabel Builderman, with whom he co-authored The Thanksgiving Manifesto. Lady Christabel is also the author of the devotional From the Desk of My Husband. Together, they are blessed with four children.

A Few Things To Note

A space for prayer & encouragement.

1

A Non-Denominational, Respectful Space

Open to Christian women of every background and belief. Share scripture and faith from a place of encouragement, not by pushing a specific denomination or debating theology.

2

Stay On Topic

This platform is for prayer, encouragement, and scripture. Please avoid business/product promotions, political or divisive debates, and chain messages, spam, or unrelated forwards.

3

Honor the Meeting Time

Our virtual prayer meeting happens every 2nd Tuesday, 7–8 PM. Please be punctual, keep your mic muted unless praying/sharing, and be mindful of time.

4

Grow With Grace

We'd love for this space to grow! Invite others by sharing the group link and encouraging them to join on their own, rather than adding people directly — out of respect for everyone's privacy and choice.

5

Moderated With Love

Our admins moderate to keep this a safe space. Please be receptive if gently reminded of these rules, and reach out to an admin directly with any concerns.
